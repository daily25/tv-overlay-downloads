Public, versioned downloads for TV Sports on Google TV / Android TV and its
native Android phone companion. Follow NRL, AFL, NBL, EPL, A-League and NFL with
real scores, fixtures and team logos.

- [TV Sports 1.11.1 APK — build 60](https://github.com/daily25/tv-overlay-downloads/releases/download/v1.11.1/tv-overlay-1.11.1.apk)

Choose **Check for updates** in the existing TV app or in the companion's
Settings. You can also install the matching APK above over the existing app;
keep the app installed to retain your settings. TV controls need no sign-in; phone linking is optional.

Both apps have redesigned sports controls. Follow whole leagues or pick
individual games. Event pop-ups use the corners, and an optional full-width
live score strip sits at the top or bottom. Quiet Glass match overlays show
team stats, clocks and crests. The phone adds light/dark themes and a clear
save bar. Every control is also available from the TV remote.
